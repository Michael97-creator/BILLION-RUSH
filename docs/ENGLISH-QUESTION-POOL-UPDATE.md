# English Language Question Pool Update

Added 40 local English Language questions to the built-in question bank. The new questions are all difficulty 4 or 5 and cover challenging adult-level spelling, grammar, punctuation, usage, subject–verb agreement, conditionals, modifiers, parallel structure, pronouns, and commonly confused words.

The AI player-question generation prompt now explicitly requests adult-level English spelling and grammar questions at hard and very-hard levels, asks for regional spelling conventions to be identified where needed, and discourages elementary vocabulary drills.
